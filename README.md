## Hi there, I'm Cindy 👋

I'm a CS and Statistics student at the University of Toronto (graduating May 2027) and a software engineer who likes systems that hold up at scale. I just finished a co-op building backend and distributed systems for a regulated fintech, and now I'm digging deeper into performance, systems, and ML.

#### 💼 Recent experience
- **Software Engineer (Co-op) @ [Tetra Digital Group](https://tetradg.com)** · May 2025 – Aug. 2026<br>

I scaled a CSV export pipeline with keyset pagination and resumable parallel partitions, built an append-only audit log service in Go on NATS JetStream, replaced manual invoicing with a Step Functions pipeline, and debugged data-consistency issues down to root cause.

#### 🔭 Currently working on
- **ChartProof**, my capstone, an independent accuracy auditor for AI medical scribes. I designed our S3 data lake and PostgreSQL warehouse, and I own the database, the loader that quarantines bad batches, and our GitHub Actions CI. I also reworked the team's Terraform to run the pipelines on ECS Fargate.
- **AI Marketing Agent**, a multi-tenant agentic platform for marketing teams. I led experiments comparing a LangGraph supervisor with a single-loop agent, which did better on outcome checks with fewer tokens, proposed replacing it, and defined a memory eval for learning from reviewer edits.
